Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

ClipBot is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer and is used only by its owner.

What ClipBot accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (the youtube.upload scope) to the owner's own channel. ClipBot does not read, change or delete any other YouTube data.
  • TikTok and Instagram: permission to upload videos to the owner's own accounts.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens and the videos ClipBot makes are stored only on the owner's computer. ClipBot has no servers. It does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone.

Services used

To pick moments for clips, video transcripts may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Uploads go directly to the platforms through their official APIs. ClipBot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in each platform's app settings. Deleting ClipBot's secrets folder removes all stored tokens.
